Chosen Problem

Environmental Pollution

Target Audience

Grade school students who want to learn about recycling and proper waste management.

Content Type

Tutorials / How-to

A simple tutorial that teaches students how to recycle and reuse materials.

Need Addressed

Students need to learn proper waste segregation, recycling techniques, and creative ways to reuse materials to help prevent environmental pollution.

Our Project Ideas

We will demonstrate how to separate waste, reuse recyclable materials, reduce plastic use, and make useful things from recycled materials.

We encourage students to practice the 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle to keep the environment clean.
